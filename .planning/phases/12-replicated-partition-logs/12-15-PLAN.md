  <behavior>
    - `reconcile_on_takeover(store, p, log)` runs when this node starts leading p (before its first upload of p): `walk_back` from the head to `chain_start`; find the first chunk (oldest-first) with `end > end_offset_for_epoch(chunk.epoch)` under the new leader's epoch history, or whose envelopes are not a prefix of the local committed log; if it straddles (base < valid end), upload a new chunk with the consistent prefix (new key because `end` differs), `prev` = the last good chunk; move the head (If-Match) to the repaired tail; enqueue every unreachable object for deletion by remote retention **after** the new head PUT returned. If nothing diverges, no write happens.
    - **Gap detection (W4):** followers trim by their own snapshot floor and never see the leader's upload floor, so a new leader's `log_start` may lie above the head's last chunk end. `reconcile_on_takeover` detects `log_start > head.end` (and also a head whose chain cannot be walked back to its `chain_start`) and **re-chains from a remote snapshot**: upload the node's newest local OSNP snapshot at S ≥ log_start to `p/<pppp>/snap/` (Phase 8 key scheme), start a new chain segment with `chain_start = S` (first chunk's `prev` = none per the 08-13 start rule), move the head (If-Match) recording the new `chain_start` and snapshot key, and let retention delete the old unreachable chain after the head is durable. The oracle `objstore::chain_equals_committed` treats the snapshot as the chain base.
    - Restore (`restore.rs`): a bucket without `manifest/current` but with `p/*/head` objects from a multi-member cluster → `RestoreError::ClusterBucketUnsupported` with the message "cluster-wide restore is deferred (Phase 13/14); see docs/replication.md".
    - Tests (`cluster_repair.rs`): a constructed divergent remote tail (old leader uploaded a chunk past the committed end at epoch 2; new leader's history ends epoch 2 earlier) is repaired: reachable chain == committed prefix byte for byte (using the 12-03 oracle logic re-implemented minimally in the test or a shared helper inside ostro-backup tests), divergent objects deleted after the head moved; a straddling chunk is re-cut; a crash (simulated by stopping after the new chunk PUT but before the head PUT) leaves the old head valid and a re-run converges; no repair when consistent. **Gap test (W4):** node B takes over p with `log_start` above the head's last chunk end (its log was trimmed while A led) → B uploads a snapshot, re-chains from it, the head points at the new chain, and the reachable chain from the snapshot equals B's committed log.
    - `cluster_mutants.rs` (`#![cfg(feature = "failpoints")]`, single-threaded): with `upload_above_hw`, a chunk containing uncommitted envelopes reaches the bucket (the test asserts the "remote ⊆ committed" property is violated under the mutant).
