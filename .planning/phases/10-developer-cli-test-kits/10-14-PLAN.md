Naming per language:
- Python snake_case: `start_run(workflow, input=None, *, run_id=None, idempotency_key=None, tags=None)`, `result(run_id, wait=None)` (wait as "30s" or seconds float).
- TS camelCase: `startRun(workflow, input, opts)`, `result(runId, {wait: "30s"})`, `devSkip({by: "72h"})`.
