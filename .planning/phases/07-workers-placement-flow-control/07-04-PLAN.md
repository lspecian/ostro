    - While paused, `enqueue_ready` (every caller: wake from Scheduled on input, executor EnqueueReady, Suspend wake-ups, retry) calls `leave_running` and `remove_ready`, sets status Ready and `rec.ready = None`, and writes **no** ready row and pushes **no** EnqueueReady.
    - While paused: EventsAppended (with or without Suspend/EnqueueReady/CompleteRun intents) and AttemptGranted → Rejected(RunPaused); nothing in the batch is applied (atomic reject).
    - While paused, inputs are journaled (TimerFired, CompleteEffect, SignalRun, CompleteToken, RequestCancel's CancelRequested) and the run is not woken.
